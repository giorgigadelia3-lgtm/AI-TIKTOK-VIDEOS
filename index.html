AI Art Factory

AI Art Factory is a private, owner-operated automation built with n8n. It generates AI-based media assets and captions and publishes them to a single owner-controlled TikTok account via the official TikTok API. This is not a public consumer app and it does not provide accounts or access to third-party users.

Purpose

Provide transparent Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for a verified domain used in TikTok app submissions.

Scope

Internal automation only. No public sign-up, no user accounts, and no third-party uploads.

Data Handling

Operational metadata and owner-granted OAuth tokens are used solely to publish content and monitor jobs.

Service Overview

The automation runs workflows in n8n to assemble AI-generated assets, apply templates, and publish finished videos to TikTok. The service is used exclusively by the account owner and is not marketed or offered to the public.

For full legal and data handling details, review the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Not a Public App

AI Art Factory does not offer accounts, subscriptions, or access for third parties. All access is limited to the owner and authorized operators.
