Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 5, 2026

1. Overview

AI Art Factory is a private, owner-operated automation built with n8n. It publishes AI-assisted content to a single owner-controlled TikTok account. The service is not offered to the public and does not provide accounts for third-party users.

2. Data We Collect

The service collects only the data required to run the automation and publish content. This may include:

  • Operational metadata such as timestamps, job status, and API response codes.
  • Workflow inputs and outputs needed to generate and publish content.
  • Owner-provided configuration values.

We do not intentionally collect personal data from the public or maintain user accounts.

3. OAuth Tokens and Credentials

Access to TikTok is granted by the account owner via OAuth. Tokens and credentials are used only to publish content and retrieve publishing status. They are stored securely and are not shared with third parties except as required to operate the service.

4. Content Handling

Media assets, prompts, and captions are processed to generate and publish posts. These materials are retained only as long as needed for operational purposes, auditing, and troubleshooting.

5. Logs and Analytics

The website does not use tracking scripts or third-party analytics by default. Basic server logs may be generated by the hosting provider for security and reliability.

6. Third-Party Services

The automation uses the TikTok API and n8n (cloud or self-hosted). Hosting and storage services may be used to deliver the site and store media if configured. These providers process data only to provide their services.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with service providers necessary to run the automation and comply with legal obligations.

8. Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect credentials and operational data, including access controls and secure storage where available.

9. Data Retention

Operational data is retained only for as long as needed to operate, troubleshoot, or comply with applicable obligations, then deleted or anonymized when no longer required.

10. Your Choices

The account owner can revoke OAuth access at any time through TikTok settings and can request deletion of stored operational data.
